About

Hi,
I'm Ben.

I'm a senior at the University of Michigan, majoring in Design and minoring in UX Design. I focus on user-driven design — building thoughtful digital experiences that balance usability, accessibility, and strong visual identity.

Art has been central to my life as long as I can remember — product design, video editing, graphic design. I love diving into creative projects and pushing new techniques.

I'm also passionate about politics and hope to combine design thinking with an entrepreneurial mindset to contribute in innovative, impactful ways.
